# NovaQuest Arena

Play games, complete quests, create block-style obby worlds, and customize your avatar with coins earned in the game.

## Avatar shop

Join with a username, then open **Avatars**. Earn coins by completing quests and playable games. Spend coins to unlock new avatar looks, and equip any look you already own. Your avatar and coin balance save in local storage on this browser/device only.

## Important demo limitations

This is a front-end prototype, not a secure multiplayer service. Accounts, coins, avatars, games, and owner actions are local to the browser and can be edited or erased by its user. They do not sync across devices or players. Email and username entry are not verified identity; do not enter a real password or use this demo for real moderation/economy.
